# Apex Market UI direction

Reference-driven redesign inspired by the information density and drill-down workflow in the user's screenshots, while keeping Apex's own visual identity and avoiding copied branding/trade dress.

Core UX goals:
- data-dense multi-sport prop cards rather than a sparse table
- sport switcher, book/app filters, market/stat filters, games filters, and sorting chips
- prop cards with player, matchup, line, best books, market dispersion, consensus, and watchlist
- drill-down player/prop sheet with book comparison, game context, line distribution, and provider freshness
- bottom navigation: Props, Players, Popular, Discrepancies, Watchlist
- no fabricated L5/L10/H2H/season/injury data; advanced statistical panels remain clearly unavailable until a real stats/history source is connected
